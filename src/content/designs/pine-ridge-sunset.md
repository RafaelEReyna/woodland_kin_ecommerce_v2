## Pine Ridge Sunset

If you've ever driven the north shore of Big Bear Lake around 7pm in the summer, you know the light we're talking about. The sun drops behind the ridge and paints everything — the pines, the granite, the water — in shades of amber and rose that you can't quite capture in a photo.

Pine Ridge Sunset is our attempt anyway. The design uses a gradient palette pulled directly from those evenings, layered over a silhouette of the treeline as seen from the Fawnskin shoreline. It's warm, it's familiar, and it hits different if you've been there.

This one's for the golden hour chasers, the dock sitters, and anyone who's ever said "just five more minutes" while watching the sky change.
