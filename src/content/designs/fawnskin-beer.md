## Fawnskin Beer

Fawnskin doesn't have much — and that's the whole point. No stoplights, no chain restaurants, just a two-lane road, a general store, and more pine trees than people. It's the quiet side of the lake, and the folks over there like it that way.

Fawnskin Beer is our ode to the north shore pace of life. The kind of afternoon where the only plan is a cold beer on the porch, the sound of the wind through the Jeffrey pines, and maybe a deer wandering through the yard. No rush. No agenda. Just here.

This one's for the Fawnskin faithful — and anyone who's ever crossed the dam and felt the whole world slow down.
