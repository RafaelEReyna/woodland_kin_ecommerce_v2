## Moonridge Both Mountains

There's a stretch of Moonridge Road where you can see both mountains at once — Bertha to the west, Gold Mountain to the east — framed by the pines like they were placed there on purpose. Locals barely notice it anymore, but visitors always slow down.

This design captures that double vista, the one that reminds you just how tucked into the mountains this town really is. It's not a postcard view. It's the everyday view — the one you see on the drive home from the Village, the one that greets you when you step outside on a cold morning.

Both Mountains is for the people who don't just visit Big Bear. It's for the ones who live between the peaks.
