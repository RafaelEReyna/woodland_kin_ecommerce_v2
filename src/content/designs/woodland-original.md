## Woodland Original

Before there were twenty designs, there was one. The Woodland Original is the wordmark that launched Woodland Kin — hand-lettered by our founding artist and refined over dozens of iterations until it felt right. Not trendy. Not trying too hard. Just the name, set in a style that could have come from a park service sign or a hand-painted cabin door.

The design philosophy behind this one is simple: if it doesn't need to be there, take it out. No extra flourishes, no hidden meanings, no limited-edition colorways. It's the brand in its purest form — a name and a feeling.

The Woodland Original is our best seller for a reason. It goes with everything, it starts conversations, and it wears in beautifully over time. This is the one you grab when you're heading out the door and want something that just works.
